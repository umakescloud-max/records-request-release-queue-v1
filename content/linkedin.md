Records requests from law firms come in through portals, fax and email, and nobody has one list of what is due.

I built a records request and release desk for injury practices that run on PIP, LOPs and workers' comp.

Every request lands in one list with the channel it came in on and the firm's due date.

When an authorization's birth date matches neither of two same-name charts, the desk flags it before any pages are pulled and counts down to the due date.

Nothing goes out to a firm until the records specialist checks the chart, date range, pages, bill and balance and clicks approve.

At about 32 minutes a request and 70 requests a week, this is roughly 37 specialist hours a week at one practice.
